Working Papers

  • Asset Purchase Rules in the Euro Area and Their Effect on Bond Markets
    with Valentin Haddad and Alan Moreira
    We study how asset purchase rules in the Euro Area affect bond markets. Central bank purchase programs create mechanical demand that affects prices, but the rules governing these purchases create predictable patterns that investors can anticipate and trade on.
  • (Why) Have Financial Markets Become More Volatile? The Role of Market Index Trading
    with Lars Lochstoer
    We examine whether the rise of index trading has contributed to increased market volatility. As more capital flows into passive strategies, price discovery may be affected, with implications for market stability.
  • Asset Purchase Rules: How QE Transformed the Bond Market
    with Valentin Haddad and Alan Moreira
    WFA Nasdaq Award, Best Paper in Asset Pricing
    Video Presentation Slides
    We show that the Federal Reserve's asset purchase programs fundamentally changed the structure of the bond market. QE created a new form of predictable demand that investors learned to anticipate, affecting bond pricing and market dynamics.
  • Diverging Banking Sector: New Facts and Macro Implications
    with Shohini Kundu and Jinyuan Zhang
    We document new facts about heterogeneity in the banking sector and explore the macroeconomic implications of this divergence.
  • Bank Fragility when Depositors are the Asset
    with Valentin Haddad and Barney Hartman-Glaser
    We develop a model where bank depositors are themselves valuable assets. This perspective changes how we think about bank runs and financial fragility.
  • Hedging Risk Factors
    with Bernard Herskovic and Alan Moreira
    R&R at Review of Financial Studies
    We study how investors can hedge exposure to systematic risk factors and the implications for portfolio construction.

Publications / Forthcoming

  • Intermediaries and Asset Prices
    with Valentin Haddad
    forthcoming, Journal of Economic Literature
    We survey the growing literature on intermediary asset pricing, discussing both theoretical foundations and empirical evidence for how financial intermediaries affect asset prices.
  • Market Macrostructure: Institutions and Asset Prices
    with Valentin Haddad
    Annual Review of Financial Economics, November 2025
    We discuss how market structure and institutional features affect asset prices, bridging microstructure and asset pricing.
  • Whatever it Takes? The Impact of Conditional Policy Promises
    with Valentin Haddad and Alan Moreira
    American Economic Review, January 2025
    We study how conditional policy promises, like Mario Draghi's famous "whatever it takes" statement, affect financial markets. These promises create contingent commitments that stabilize markets without necessarily requiring actual intervention.
  • How Credit Cycles Across a Financial Crisis
    with Arvind Krishnamurthy
    Journal of Finance, March 2025
    We document how credit conditions evolve around financial crises, showing distinct patterns in the buildup and aftermath of crisis episodes.
  • 1930: First Modern Crisis
    with Gary Gorton and Toomas Laarits
    Financial History Review, April 2024
    We argue that 1930 represents the first modern financial crisis, sharing key features with subsequent crises that earlier panics lacked.
  • Volatility Expectations and Returns
    with Lars Lochstoer
    Journal of Finance, April 2022
    We study how volatility expectations relate to future returns, finding evidence that investors underreact to volatility information.
  • Intermediaries and Asset Prices: Evidence from the U.S., U.K., and Japan, 1870-2016
    with Matt Baron
    Review of Financial Studies, May 2022
    We construct long historical time series of intermediary capital ratios for multiple countries and study their relationship with asset prices across 150 years of data.
  • When Selling Becomes Viral: Disruptions in Debt Markets in the COVID-19 Crisis and the Fed's Response
    with Valentin Haddad and Alan Moreira
    Review of Financial Studies, October 2021
    AFA Presentation Slides
    We study the dramatic disruptions in corporate bond and Treasury markets during March 2020 and the Federal Reserve's unprecedented response. We show how selling pressure cascaded through markets and analyze the effectiveness of Fed interventions.
  • Do Intermediaries Matter for Aggregate Asset Prices?
    with Valentin Haddad
    Journal of Finance, December 2021
    Winner, 2022 Journal of Finance DFA Prize, First Place
    NBER Presentation
    We test whether financial intermediaries matter for aggregate asset prices by examining how shocks to intermediary capital affect the pricing of risk across different asset classes.
  • Mobile Collateral vs Immobile Collateral
    with Gary Gorton and Toomas Laarits
    Journal of Money Credit and Banking, September 2022
    Bloomberg
    We study the distinction between mobile and immobile collateral and its implications for financial markets and monetary policy.
  • Is Risk Mispriced in a Credit Boom?
    Prepared for the INET Private Debt Initiative
    We examine whether risk is systematically mispriced during credit booms, with implications for macroprudential policy.
  • Should Long-Term Investors Time Volatility?
    with Alan Moreira
    Journal of Financial Economics, March 2019 [Lead Article]
    We show that even long-term investors should time volatility by reducing equity exposure when volatility is high. This strategy improves risk-adjusted returns without requiring market timing ability.
  • Volatility-Managed Portfolios
    with Alan Moreira
    Journal of Finance, August 2017
    NBER Presentation Slides Internet Appendix
    Financial Times FT 2018 CNBC USA Today
    We show that managing portfolio volatility significantly improves the Sharpe ratios of factor portfolios. Scaling exposure inversely with recent volatility is a simple strategy that benefits from the low-risk anomaly.
  • Financial Crises and Risk Premia
    Quarterly Journal of Economics, May 2017
    I show that risk premia rise dramatically during financial crises, and this increase in risk premia is a key mechanism through which financial crises affect the real economy.
  • Aggregate External Financing and Savings Waves
    with Andrea Eisfeldt
    Journal of Monetary Economics, December 2016
    We study the aggregate patterns of external financing by firms and document "savings waves" where firms collectively build up cash reserves.
  • Financial Intermediaries and the Cross-Section of Asset Returns
    with Tobias Adrian and Erkko Etula
    Journal of Finance, December 2014
    Winner, 2015 Journal of Finance Amundi Smith Breeden Prize, Distinguished Paper
    Utah WFC Video Slides Web Appendix Data
    NBER discussion of He, Kelly, Manela (2017)
    We show that the leverage of financial intermediaries is a strong predictor of expected returns across asset classes. This finding supports intermediary-based asset pricing models where intermediary constraints affect risk premia.
